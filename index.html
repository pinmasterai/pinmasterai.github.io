The difference

Finally, the one number nobody else shows you

Other tools count clicks and impressions. PinMaster AI follows the money — all the way down to the individual pin that earned it.

Every pin, its own profit line

ClickBank and Digistore24 send a postback for every sale, tagged to the exact pin that produced it. You open one screen and see precisely what each pin is worth — no spreadsheets, no guessing.

Refunds come back out — automatically

A sale that gets refunded or charged back is subtracted from that pin’s total the moment the network reports it. The number you see is the money you actually keep, never the inflated version.

So you clone winners, not maybes

Once you know your top pins by real earnings, you duplicate what works and stop feeding what doesn’t. Your next month of content is built on proof, not hunches.
